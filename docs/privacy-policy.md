# Blurt! Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

Blurt! is a party game that runs entirely on your device.

**We collect no data.** Blurt! has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It
does not connect to the internet during play, and the store builds don't request network access.

**What stays on your device.** The game saves your settings, the phrases you've already seen (so
they don't repeat), any custom packs you make, and any player and team names you type in. This
data never leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes it, and you can clear phrase history in
Settings.

**Sharing custom packs.** If you share a custom pack, the app turns it into a code or QR code
that you choose to send. We never receive it.

**Children.** Blurt! collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this ever changes (for example, if an optional tip jar is added), this policy and
the store privacy labels will be updated before the release that changes it.
